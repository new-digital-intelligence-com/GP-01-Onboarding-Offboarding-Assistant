# NDI onboarding handbook — extract

*Sample policy source for GP-01's Q&A mode. Dummy content for demo purposes. In a real
tenant this is the People Ops handbook folder named in the settings doc.*

## First week

Your manager runs a welcome session on day one at 10:00 local time and a check-in at the
end of week one. People Ops runs a company induction every second Tuesday at 14:00 CET —
you are enrolled automatically for the next one after your start date.

## Equipment

IT ships your laptop to the address you gave HR, to arrive before your start date. If it
has not arrived by 17:00 the working day before you start, mail it-support@ndi.example.
Phones are issued only for client-facing roles; ask your manager if you think you need one.

## Access and accounts

Your accounts are created before your first day. If something is missing on day one, tell
your manager rather than requesting it yourself — access follows your role, and requests
outside your role need an approver.

Password manager use is mandatory for every shared credential. Personal accounts and
company accounts must not be mixed.

## Working hours and leave

Standard hours are 40 per week, arranged with your team. Core collaboration hours are
10:00–15:00 CET.

Annual leave is 30 days for full-time employees in the Nordics entity and 28 days in the
German entity, plus public holidays for your location. Leave is requested in the HR system
and approved by your manager. In your first three months, leave of more than five
consecutive days needs your manager's agreement in advance.

Sick leave is reported to your manager on the first morning of absence. A medical
certificate is required from day four in the Nordics entity.

## Expenses

Expenses are submitted monthly in the HR system with a receipt attached. Travel over
€500 needs written approval before booking. Client-billable expenses must reference the
engagement code.

## Remote work

NDI is remote-first. Teams agree their own office days. Working from another country for
more than 20 working days a year needs People Ops approval in advance because of tax and
social-security rules — start that conversation early.

## Who to ask

| Topic | Who |
|---|---|
| Access, accounts, equipment | it-support@ndi.example |
| Policy, leave, contracts, payroll | people-ops@ndi.example |
| Your work, priorities, clients | your manager |
| Anything about pay, performance or immigration | People Ops directly, never a shared channel |
